FAQ

Questions people actually ask

Do I have to link my bank account?+

No. Every number is entered manually — PayWindow doesn't require your bank login, and there are no linked accounts.

What if I'm not paid every two weeks?+

PayWindow supports weekly, biweekly, semi-monthly, and monthly schedules — your budget windows follow your actual pay dates, not a fixed calendar month. After a base income cadence is set, one-time income entries are also supported.

What if I have no set pay schedule?+

Choose a base rhythm to plan around (for example, every two weeks), change the amount for any pay window to match what you actually earned, and add one-time income as it comes in. It takes a little setup, but it works.

Is my data private?+

Your budget data is stored on your device, and there are no accounts, so nothing about your finances sits on a server. The one exception: if you take the optional free-trial survey, your anonymous answers are sent to us.
